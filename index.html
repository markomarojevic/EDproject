    Info
    
      
    
        
    Expressive Touch is a musical instrument you play by tilting your phone.
    
        
    How to play
    
        
      
          
    1. On the Home screen, tap the blob to turn on sound and the tilt sensor. Tap it again to pause or resume playing.
      2. 
          
    2. Hold the phone comfortably and press Calibrate — that is the neutral position. The Sensitivity slider sets how far you need to tilt.
      3. 
          
    3. Tilt the phone up, down, left or right. Each direction plays one sound. Return to the middle before the next hit.
      4. 
          
    4. In the Sound Map, tap sounds to preview them and Confirm one for the selected direction. ☆ saves the combination to your favorites.
      5. 
          
    5. In the Studio, record an 8-second layer (after a 3-2-1 countdown) and stack layers on top of each other.
            ▶ Play all plays all unmuted layers, and ▶ next to a layer plays only that layer.
      6. 
        
    
        
    Tilting plays sounds only on the Home screen and while recording a layer in the Studio.
    
        
    About
    
        
    First prototype for the Interaction Design course. All sounds are synthesized in the browser (Web Audio API).
    
        
    On a computer, the arrow keys replace tilting.
